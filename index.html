Who We Are

Based in Benin.
Building Across
Nigeria.

Tegz Architecture & Construction Company is a privately held Nigerian construction firm headquartered on PZ Road, Benin, Edo State. We bring together architecture, civil engineering, and construction management under one roof.

Our two divisions — Civil Works Group and Building Group — allow us to specialise deeply, whether we're piling foundations, erecting steel frames, installing HVAC systems, or delivering complete turnkey buildings for the hospitality, healthcare, education, and sports sectors.

✓ Privately held, 11–50 strong specialist team
✓ Design-build capability across civil and architectural sectors
✓ HVAC, plumbing, electrical, piling and excavation in-house
