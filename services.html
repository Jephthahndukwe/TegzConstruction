What We Deliver

Our Services

Two focused divisions delivering infrastructure and architectural construction across Nigeria from our Benin, Edo headquarters.

Jump to: Civil Works Group Building Group MEP Services Piling & Excavation
Civil Works Group

Public Infrastructure

The Civil Works Group handles Nigeria's public infrastructure needs — from federal road contracts to state-level bridge rehabilitation, drainage systems, airport works, and marine structures.

Civil Works

Highways & Road Works

From new carriageway construction to road rehabilitation and maintenance, our civil teams deliver to federal and state specification. We self-perform excavation, sub-base works, drainage, and surface courses.

  • New road construction and dual carriageways
  • Road rehabilitation and overlay works
  • Drainage channels and culverts
  • Road markings, signage, and kerbing
  • Sub-base and base-course compaction
Civil Works

Bridges & Structures

Bridge construction and rehabilitation demands structural precision and deep understanding of geotechnical conditions. Our civil engineers and piling specialists work together on every structural waterway crossing.

  • New bridge construction (concrete and steel)
  • Bridge rehabilitation and deck replacement
  • Abutment and pier works
  • Marine and riverine structures
  • Structural condition surveys
Building Group

Architectural Construction

The Building Group delivers full architectural construction across hospitality, sports, education, healthcare, industrial, and residential sectors. From design-build mandates to general contracting, we bring projects from ground-breaking to occupation.

Building Group

Hospitality & Commercial

Hotels, resorts, office towers, retail centres, and mixed-use developments. We manage the complexity of commercial construction — tight urban sites, multiple trades, and fast-track programmes — without sacrificing quality.

  • Hotels and hospitality construction
  • Office towers and commercial complexes
  • Retail and mixed-use development
  • Full MEP coordination and installation
  • Fit-out and interior works
Building Group

Education, Sports & Healthcare

Schools, universities, hospitals, clinics, and sports facilities require functional precision. Tegz understands the technical requirements of each sector and delivers compliant, purpose-built environments.

  • School and university construction
  • Hospitals and primary healthcare centres
  • Sports halls, stadia, and courts
  • Special facility fit-outs (labs, operating theatres)
  • Government and institutional buildings
Building Group

Residential Construction

From individual bespoke homes to large-scale housing estate development, we handle every aspect of residential construction. Our team self-performs structural, MEP, and finishing works for single-source accountability.

  • Luxury residential homes and villas
  • Housing estate development
  • Multi-storey apartment blocks
  • Renovation and extension works
  • Estate infrastructure (roads, drainage, utilities)
In-House Capability

MEP Services
HVAC · Plumbing · Electrical

We bring mechanical, electrical, and plumbing works in-house — not subcontracted. One point of accountability, proper coordination between trades, and better outcomes for the building owner.

Our HVAC teams handle central air conditioning, ventilation, and refrigeration systems. Our plumbing crews manage water supply, drainage, and sanitation. Our electricians cover power distribution, lighting, and specialist systems.

Fire & BMS

Fire detection, suppression systems, and building management system integration.

Ground Engineering

Piling & Excavation

Every stable structure begins below grade. Tegz operates piling and excavation equipment in-house for driven, bored, and mini piles — critical in Edo's varied ground conditions.

  • Bored and driven piling
  • Mini piles and micro piles
  • Bulk excavation and basement works
  • Soil investigation and reporting
  • Retaining walls and sheet piling
