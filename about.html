Our Story

Who We Are

A Nigerian construction firm rooted in Benin, Edo — delivering civil infrastructure and architectural construction with integrity, technical precision, and a commitment to lasting quality.

Our Foundation

Bringing Construction
Visions to Life

Tegz Architecture & Construction Company was founded with a straightforward ambition: to provide Nigeria with a construction firm that combines deep technical expertise with genuine reliability. Starting from Benin, Edo State, we have grown into a multi-disciplinary firm with specialist capability across civil works and architectural construction.

The name "Tegz" reflects our founder Tega Obire's personal commitment to every project we undertake. We are a privately held company — 11 to 50 specialists strong — which means every client gets direct attention from our leadership, not a subcontractor chain.

Today we operate through two focused divisions: the Civil Works Group, delivering public infrastructure including highways, bridges, airports, and marine structures; and the Building Group, specialising in hospitality, sports, education, healthcare, industrial, and residential construction.

Our Journey

Milestones That
Shaped Tegz

2010

Company Founded

Tegz Architecture & Construction Company established by Tega Obire in Benin, Edo State, with a focus on general contracting and building works.

2014

Civil Works Division Launched

Created the dedicated Civil Works Group to serve public sector infrastructure contracts — roads, drainage, and bridge works in Edo and Delta States.

2018

MEP Capability Expanded

Brought HVAC, plumbing, and electrical installation fully in-house, enabling complete turnkey delivery for commercial and institutional clients.

2021

Building Group Formalised

Formalised the Building Group division to address the growing demand from the hospitality, healthcare, education, and sports construction sectors.

Today

120+ Projects, Still Growing

Tegz continues to expand its portfolio across Nigeria, with a strong pipeline of civil and building projects from government and private clients.
